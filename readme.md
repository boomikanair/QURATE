# QURATE 📚

### Question Paper Analyzer

> **Turn previous-year question papers into a smarter study plan.**

QURATE is an AI-powered web application that analyzes previous-year question papers and helps students understand **what to study, what matters most, and what to practice.**

---

## ✨ What QURATE Does

Upload your previous-year question papers and QURATE will:

- 📄 Extract questions automatically from PDFs
- 🎯 Detect the subject
- 🧠 Identify important academic topics
- 🔗 Group similar questions and concepts
- 📊 Analyze topic frequency and marks
- 🔥 Identify high-weightage topics
- 📌 Tell you what to study first
- 🔮 Generate likely expected questions
- ✍️ Generate additional practice questions
